# Presentation Templates

> Slide-by-slide structures for each presentation type.
> Based on 10-20-30 (Kawasaki), Duarte storytelling, Pyramid Principle.

---

## Pitch Deck (Investors) — 14 slides, 20 min

| # | Action Title | Content | Time |
|---|-------------|---------|------|
| 1 | [Company] — [Mission one-liner] | Logo, tagline, contact. Mission = why you exist | 30s |
| 2 | [X million people] face [problem] every day | Pain point quantified. Make it visceral | 2m |
| 3 | The market is ready: [why now] | Macro shift, regulation, technology change | 1m |
| 4 | [Product] solves this by [core mechanism] | Solution framed as value proposition | 2m |
| 5 | Here's how it works | Demo / product screenshots / video | 3m |
| 6 | [Metric] proves people want this | Traction: revenue, users, growth rate, retention | 2m |
| 7 | A $X billion opportunity | TAM/SAM/SOM with methodology stated | 1.5m |
| 8 | We make money by [model] | Revenue model, pricing, unit economics | 1.5m |
| 9 | We reach customers through [channel] | GTM strategy, CAC, channel mix | 2m |
| 10 | We're positioned to win against [competitors] | 2x2 matrix or feature comparison | 1.5m |
| 11 | Built by [team credentials] | Why THIS team. Highlight relevant exits, domain expertise | 1.5m |
| 12 | Projected path to [milestone] | Financial projections with key assumptions | 1m |
| 13 | Raising $X to reach [milestone] | Use of funds breakdown, runway, next inflection point | 1m |
| 14 | Join us in [vision] | CTA: specific ask + how to follow up | 30s |

## Sales Pitch — 7 slides, 15 min

| # | Action Title | Content | Time |
|---|-------------|---------|------|
| 1 | [Your brand] — helping [audience] achieve [outcome] | Logo + one-liner about THEIR outcome | 30s |
| 2 | [X%] of [audience] struggle with [problem] | Their pain, not your product. Quantified. | 2m |
| 3 | [Product] delivers [benefit] through [mechanism] | Benefits > features. 3 key benefits max. | 2m |
| 4 | Why [product] over [alternative] | USPs, comparison, unfair advantage | 2m |
| 5 | [Client] achieved [result] with [product] | Case study or testimonial with numbers | 2.5m |
| 6 | Plans starting at [price] for [value] | Pricing tiers, clear and simple | 2m |
| 7 | Let's get you started: [next step] | CTA: trial, meeting, proposal timeline | 30s |

## Board / Corporate Meeting — 8 slides, 30 min (20 + Q&A)

| # | Action Title | Content | Time |
|---|-------------|---------|------|
| 1 | Today's decision: [recommendation] | Purpose of meeting + SCQA opening | 1m |
| 2 | Executive summary: [situation → complication → resolution] | SCR on one slide. Bold claims, indented evidence | 2m |
| 3 | [Key metric] shows [trend/insight] | Data slide with action title. Chart + callout. | 3m |
| 4 | [Second metric] reveals [opportunity/risk] | Second data point proving the argument | 3m |
| 5 | [Analysis] supports [recommendation] | Supporting evidence (may be multiple slides) | 4m |
| 6 | Recommendation: [specific action] | Multi-column: argument + evidence + metric per column | 2m |
| 7 | Next steps: [action] by [owner] by [date] | Table: Action / Owner / Deadline / Dependencies | 1m |
| 8+ | Appendix | Backup data, only accessed if asked | Q&A |

## Educational / Training — 7 slides, 20 min

| # | Action Title | Content | Time |
|---|-------------|---------|------|
| 1 | Today you'll learn [skill/concept] | Hook: provocative question or surprising statistic | 1m |
| 2 | [Concept 1]: [what it means in plain language] | First key concept. Visual > text. | 3m |
| 3 | [Concept 2]: [why it matters] | Second concept. Build on first. | 3m |
| 4 | [Concept 3]: [how to apply it] | Third concept. Practical application. | 3m |
| 5 | See it in action: [real example] | Case study, worked example, or live demo | 4m |
| 6 | Try it yourself: [exercise] | Interactive element. Pair/share, worksheet, or poll | 4m |
| 7 | Your 3 takeaways: [1, 2, 3] | Summary. Last thing = most remembered. | 2m |

## Conference / TEDx Talk — 9 slides, 18 min

| # | Action Title | Content | Time |
|---|-------------|---------|------|
| 1 | [Opening story or statistic] | Hook. No title slide — start IN the story. "What is" | 2m |
| 2 | This is [problem] — and it affects [audience] | Make it personal to the audience. "What is" | 2m |
| 3 | But what if [possibility]? | First shift. "What could be" [CONTRAST] | 2m |
| 4 | The reality is [complexity/tension] | Back to reality. "What is" | 1.5m |
| 5 | I discovered [insight] through [experience] | Personal story. "What could be" [CONTRAST] | 3m |
| 6 | [Evidence/research] confirms this | Data point or study. Credibility. | 2m |
| 7 | Imagine a world where [new bliss] | "New bliss" — world with your idea adopted | 2m |
| 8 | Here's what you can do today: [CTA] | Specific, actionable ask | 1m |
| 9 | [Closing line] | The line they'll quote. No "thank you" — end on impact. | 30s |

## Academic Defense — 12 slides, 20 min

| # | Action Title | Content | Time |
|---|-------------|---------|------|
| 1 | [Research question]: [concise version] | Title + research question + your name | 30s |
| 2 | [Field] lacks understanding of [gap] | Literature gap — what we don't know yet | 2m |
| 3 | This study asks: [question] via [method] | Research question + methodology overview | 1.5m |
| 4 | [Method] was chosen because [justification] | Detailed method, sample, instruments | 2m |
| 5 | [Finding 1]: [result in plain language] | First result with data visualization | 2.5m |
| 6 | [Finding 2]: [result contradicts/supports prior] | Second result, connected to literature | 2.5m |
| 7 | [Finding 3]: [unexpected/significant result] | Third result, the strongest or most surprising | 2.5m |
| 8 | These findings suggest [interpretation] | Discussion — what does it all mean? | 2m |
| 9 | [Limitation] must be acknowledged | Shows rigor. Address before committee asks. | 1m |
| 10 | Future research should [direction] | Next steps for the field | 1m |
| 11 | In summary: [3 key contributions] | Contribution to theory, practice, and method | 1.5m |
| 12 | Thank you — questions? | Contact info, open for committee | 30s |

## Custom Template

When no template fits, build from these universal slide types:

| Slide Type | Purpose | Used in |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Hook** | Capture attention in first 30s | All |
| **Problem** | Define the pain/opportunity | All |
| **Solution** | Present your answer | All |
| **Evidence** | Prove your argument with data | All |
| **Demo** | Show, don't tell | Pitch, Sales |
| **Comparison** | Position vs alternatives | Pitch, Sales |
| **Team** | Why you/your team | Pitch |
| **Financials** | Numbers and projections | Pitch, Board |
| **Timeline** | Milestones past and future | Pitch, Board |
| **CTA** | What you want them to do | All |
| **Appendix** | Backup for Q&A | Board, Academic |

Pick 7-14 slide types, order them with Duarte contrast pattern, add action titles.
