# Frameworks Quick Reference

> One-page cheat sheet for the 4 frameworks used in presentation building.

---

## 1. 10-20-30 Rule (Guy Kawasaki)

| Rule | Guideline | Why |
|------|-----------|-----|
| 10 slides | Maximum 10-16 slides | Cognitive load limit |
| 20 minutes | Main content ≤20 min | Attention span |
| 30pt font | Minimum font size | Readability forces conciseness |

**Modern update:** Average successful deck is 16 slides. Add "Mission", "Why now?", separate "Use of funds".

---

## 2. Duarte Storytelling (Resonate)

**Core principle:** The audience is the hero. Define their transformation.

```
"What is" ─── "What could be" ─── "What is" ─── "What could be" ─── "New bliss"
```

- **What is:** Current reality, shared understanding, build credibility
- **What could be:** Possibility, tension, ask reality to be different
- **New bliss:** World with your idea adopted — end here, they remember last

**3 questions before building:**
1. Who are they when they walk in?
2. Who do I want them to be when they leave?
3. Does every slide support that transformation?

---

## 3. SCQA (Pyramid Principle Opening)

| Element | What | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| **S**ituation | What they already know | "We grew 20% for 5 years" |
| **C**omplication | What changed / what's at stake | "Growth slowing to 5%" |
| **Q**uestion | The question this raises | "How do we sustain growth?" |
| **A**nswer | Your recommendation | "Enter German market in Q3" |

Takes 60 seconds. Gives context for everything that follows.

---

## 4. Action Title Rule

Every slide title states a **conclusion**, not a topic.

| ❌ Label (weak) | ✅ Action title (strong) |
|------------------|--------------------------|
| "Revenue by Quarter" | "Revenue grew 15% in Q4, signaling turnaround" |
| "Customer Satisfaction" | "NPS dropped 18 points after pricing change" |
| "Team" | "A team with 3 successful exits and 40 years experience" |
| "Market Size" | "A $4.2B market growing 12% annually" |
| "Competitors" | "3 competitors are vulnerable in adjacent segments" |

**Test:** Can someone understand the deck by reading ONLY the slide titles? If yes → good structure.

## Composition Formula

```
1. Define: AUDIENCE + TRANSFORMATION + CTA
2. Open: SCQA (60 seconds)
3. Body: Alternate contrast (Duarte pattern)
4. Evidence: 3 strongest points, action-titled
5. Close: "New bliss" + specific CTA (never "Questions?")
6. Verify: 10-20-30 rule + checklist
```

---

## 60-Second Pre-Flight Check

- [ ] Does every slide have ONE idea?
- [ ] Does every title state a conclusion?
- [ ] Is font ≥30pt?
- [ ] Is content ≤20 min?
- [ ] Does it end with a specific CTA (not "Questions?")?
- [ ] Can the deck be understood from titles alone?
