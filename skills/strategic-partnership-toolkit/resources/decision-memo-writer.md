# Decision Memo Writer

## Purpose

Turn strategic analysis into a memo that asks for a decision.

This skill is for action, not documentation. A good memo should make it clear what decision is needed, why now, what options exist, what risks remain, and what the recommended next step is.

## Required inputs

Useful inputs:
- Context.
- Decision needed.
- Options.
- Recommendation.
- Risks.
- Timeline.
- Owners.
- Evidence available.
- Open questions.

If inputs are incomplete, produce a draft with explicit assumptions.

## Method

1. Define the decision.
   One sentence:
   "We need to decide whether to..."

2. Define why now.
   Include:
   - External opportunity.
   - Deadline.
   - Partner window.
   - Patient need.
   - Institutional risk of inaction.
   - Evidence gap.

3. Summarize context.
   Keep it factual. Do not write marketing copy.

4. Present options.
   At least three:
   - Do nothing / defer.
   - Narrow pilot.
   - Broader initiative.

5. Recommend one option.
   Recommendation must include:
   - Scope.
   - Owner.
   - Timeline.
   - Required approvals.
   - Evidence expected.

6. State risks and mitigations.
   No hiding tradeoffs.

7. Define decision ask.
   End with the exact approval or alignment requested.

## Output format

### Title

Decision memo title.

### Decision required

One sentence.

### Recommendation

One paragraph.

### Why now

Short paragraph.

### Context

Factual background.

### Options

Table:
Option | Description | Pros | Cons | Risk level

### Recommended scope

What is included and excluded.

### Governance and approvals

Who must be involved before execution.

### Risks and mitigations

Table:
Risk | Mitigation | Owner

### Decision ask

Specific decision requested.

### Next 14 days

Concrete action list.

## Style rules

- Serious, plain language.
- No hype.
- No claims beyond evidence.
- No passive ambiguity.
- No fake certainty.
- No “transformational” unless justified by evidence.

## Quality gates

Before finalizing, verify:
- The memo asks for a real decision.
- The recommendation is narrower than the ambition.
- The risks are visible.
- The next steps have owners.
- The wording is safe for institutional circulation.
